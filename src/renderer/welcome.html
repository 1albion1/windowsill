Windowsill

Your cats live on your desktop.

Everything lives in your tray

Look for this cat near the clock. Pausing, adding cats and quitting all happen from its menu — Windowsill has no window of its own.

Windows 11 hides new tray icons. Click the ^ arrow next to the clock to find it, then drag the cat down onto the taskbar so it stays visible.

1

Cut your cat out of a photo

Open a photo in Paint, click Remove background, then Save as → PNG. No other software needed.

2

Name the file after what your cat is doing

Make a folder named after your cat and put the photos in it:

Mochi/
    sit.png
    walk-left.png
    sleep-right.png

The filename is the instruction. -left or -right says which way your cat faces in that photo — without it a left-facing cat walks backwards.

One photo is enough to start. A folder with just sit.png gives you a working cat. Sizes are worked out automatically.

3

Reload

Right-click the tray icon and choose Reload cats. Yours appear.
